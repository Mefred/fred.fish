PROJECT / NETWORKING

Reef

A focused workspace for connecting to and working with Cisco network devices.

Initial platform: IOS XE

Reef starts with Cisco IOS XE. Platform-specific behavior and capabilities will be kept explicit as support grows.

Project overview

In development

What it is

Reef is being built around a clear separation between device connection, device information, and configuration workflows.

Planned workflow

Connect to a device first. Once connected, show relevant device details and make available settings and operations easy to navigate.

Starting scope

Begin with dependable IOS XE support. Additional platforms and capabilities can be introduced without complicating the initial workflow.
