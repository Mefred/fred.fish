PROJECT / NETWORKING

Reef

A focused workspace for connecting to and working with Cisco network devices.

Project overview

In development

What it is

Reef is being built around Cisco IOS XE first, with a clear separation between device connection, device information, and configuration workflows.

Planned workflow

Connect to a device first. Once connected, show the relevant device details and make its available settings and operations easy to navigate.

Starting scope

Begin with IOS XE support and a dependable foundation. Additional platforms and capabilities can be introduced without making the initial interface or workflow unnecessarily complicated.
