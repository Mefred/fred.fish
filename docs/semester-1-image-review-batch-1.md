# Semester 1 image review — batch 1

**Review scope:** first 36 unique unresolved question-candidate images in source-reference order.  
**Review method:** original recovered assets placed on 960×540-per-image contact sheets; the three answer-marked slides were also opened individually at native resolution.  
**Purpose:** identify visible answer-selection marks, diagram-dependent evidence, and false-positive lesson-summary slides without inferring an answer where the source does not mark one.

## Results

- 36 candidate images screened at high-resolution contact-sheet scale.
- Three images show clear answer marks and are already linked to active source-origin questions.
- The other 33 show unmarked prompts, matching templates with blank targets, lesson-summary/explanatory slides, or unmarked diagrams. They do not provide a new unambiguous answer key.
- No new questions or source-origin associations were added. The existing totals remain 36 source-origin image-question associations and nine visual-context references for AI-generated questions (45 total).
- The original OCR ZIP's question-candidates.jsonl status fields were not rewritten. This review is recorded in the repository audit instead.
- This batch is a high-resolution contact-sheet screen, not a substitute for native-resolution review of every remaining diagram or small-text exhibit. The native-resolution answer checks are explicitly noted below.
