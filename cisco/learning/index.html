A structured place for explanations, study notes, diagrams, and topic reviews. The goal is to connect networking theory with what you actually configure and observe.

Content structure

Each topic can grow into a lesson with key concepts, examples, command output, common mistakes, a practical task, and review questions. The sections below establish that structure; they are not presented as completed lessons yet.
