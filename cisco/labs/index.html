Labs and exercises

A place for practical tasks that turn concepts into working configurations. Labs should be repeatable, verifiable, and clear about what a successful result looks like.

Lab format

Each lab will include objectives, prerequisites, a topology, addressing information, tasks, verification commands, expected results, and troubleshooting prompts.
